Privacy Policy

BLKGRL Hair Archive · Last updated 18 November 2026

BLKGRL Hair Archive is a hair calendar app for iPhone. It is built so that your information never leaves your phone. This policy explains what that means.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not have a server, so there is nowhere for your information to go.

What stays on your phone

  • The styles you plan, with their dates, costs, stylists, notes and favourites.
  • Any photos you choose to add. The app keeps its own copy of each photo inside the app, on your phone only. It only sees the photos you pick.

If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or your computer, the app's data is included in that backup, which Apple or you control, not us.

Photos permission

The app asks to open your photo library only when you choose to add a photo to a style. It cannot see any photo you don't choose.

Sharing

When you share your calendar picture, the app makes the image on your phone and hands it to the iPhone share sheet. Where it goes after that is up to you and the app you share it with.

Payments

The full version is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details, name or Apple Account. Apple's privacy policy covers that purchase: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. If the app ever started collecting information, we would say so here and in the App Store before it happened.
